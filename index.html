I'm a visual artist and digital designer who moves between screens, slides and fabric.

I'm based in Makkah, with a Bachelor's in Visual Arts from Umm Al-Qura University and a Master's in progress in the same field.

I design presentations and interactive decks, interfaces and social media posts, and I keep a hand-made practice in punch needle embroidery and textile painting. Each side feeds the other: art gives the design its feeling, and design gives the art its clarity.

    AI in my workflow. I use AI tools for research, early ideas and exploring visual directions. The concept, the visual decisions and the final polish are mine, and on hand-made work the craft is fully by hand.
